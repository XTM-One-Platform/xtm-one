!!! note "Release notes"
    Please, be sure to also take a look at the [XTM One release notes](https://github.com/XTM-One-Platform/xtm-one/releases), they may contain important information about releases and deployments.

## Introduction

XTM One is the core platform of the XTM Platform. OpenCTI (threat intelligence), OpenAEV (adversarial exposure validation) and OpenCRQ (governance, risk and compliance) are its modules: they connect to XTM One, which brings them a shared view of your exposure, common sign-in and integrations, and AI agents that work across all of them. XTM One is open source and AI-native: it lets organizations deploy autonomous agents for threat exposure management, email triage, messaging support, knowledge management and operational automation.

!!! info "Docker deployment of the full XTM suite (OpenCTI – OpenAEV – XTM One)"
    If you're looking for information about the deployment of the full eXtended Threat Management (XTM) suite using Docker, please refer [to this repository and documentation](https://github.com/FiligranHQ/xtm-docker).
